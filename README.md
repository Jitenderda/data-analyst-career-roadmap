# Data Analyst Career Roadmap

> A structured, practical learning journey to build job-ready Data Analytics skills through lessons, assignments, business case studies, and portfolio projects.

## About This Roadmap

I am an MIS Executive with 10+ years of experience working with Excel, operational data, customer enquiries, complaint tracking, dealer support, and GPS & IoT activation data in the electric three-wheeler industry.

My goal is to transition into a professional Data Analyst role by strengthening my technical skills, analytical thinking, and ability to turn business data into actionable insights.

This repository documents that learning journey. It is a separate learning track from my **Data Analytics Career Accelerator Program**.

## How I Will Learn

Each topic will follow a practical learning cycle:

1. **Learn** — Understand the concept and why it matters.
2. **Practise** — Solve focused exercises independently.
3. **Apply** — Work with realistic business datasets.
4. **Analyse** — Explain findings and identify business implications.
5. **Build** — Create projects that demonstrate the skill.
6. **Review** — Improve the work using feedback and evidence.

## Practical Business Context

Where appropriate, exercises and projects will use scenarios related to:

- Customer and dealer enquiry management
- Helpline and complaint analysis
- GPS and IoT activation performance
- Operational reporting and KPI monitoring
- Electric vehicle business performance

All portfolio datasets will be appropriately prepared for sharing; confidential company or customer information will not be published.

## Repository Structure

Learning materials will be organised by phase. Each phase may contain lessons, practice exercises, assignments, and projects as the journey progresses.

## Progress and Standards

A task is not considered complete merely because it has been attempted. Completion means the work has been submitted, checked against the requirements, and improved where necessary.

The focus is on building independent problem-solving ability—not simply collecting certificates or copying solutions.
